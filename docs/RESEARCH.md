# Research notes (Oct 2026)

- DPDP Rules: Consent Manager provisions from 14 Nov 2026; substantive duties (consent, security safeguards, data principal rights, breach reporting) from 14 May 2027. Source: https://www.getastra.com/blog/compliance/dpdp-compliance/
- Full enforcement around 13–14 May 2027 after the 18-month transition. Source: https://india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-compliance-timeline-enforcement-2026-27-44740.html
- Engineering duties: immutable consent logs (timestamp, notice version), data register, erasure when purpose ends or consent is withdrawn, breach report to the Board within 72 hours, logs kept at least 1 year.
- Penalties per instance: ₹250 cr (security safeguards), ₹200 cr (breach notice, children's data), ₹150 cr (Significant Data Fiduciary), ₹50 cr (other).

## Competitors
- OneTrust, Securiti: enterprise, legal-side.
- Indian consent/compliance platforms and checklists: policy-level, not code.
- Bearer CLI: open-source data-flow scanner, GDPR-focused, no DPDP mapping.

**Gap:** a developer-first, code-level DPDP scanner for JS/TS teams in India.
